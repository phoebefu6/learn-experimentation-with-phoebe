# Learn Experimentation with Phoebe

A two-track, interactive course on **experimentation, A/B testing, and causal inference** - the
discipline of proving whether a change actually caused a result.

**Live site:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-experimentation-with-phoebe/

## Two tracks, one running brand

- **Leader track (6 x 45 min, no code)** - for executives, PMs, and marketers. How to read a
  results deck, trust the right number, spot an underpowered or peeked-at test, and build a
  culture that experiments instead of guessing.
- **Builder track (10 x 45 min, Python)** - for DA / DE / DS. Design and analyze experiments
  from scratch: potential outcomes, power and sample size, randomization and trust, A/B and
  multivariate testing, sequential testing and the peeking problem, CUPED variance reduction,
  geo experiments and synthetic control, difference-in-differences, and observational causal
  inference.

Every session works on **Lumen Skincare**, a synthetic $18M direct-to-consumer brand whose
flagship test asks: does a new product-page layout lift checkout from 3.2% to 3.6%?

## Live layer

Two in-browser simulators (no dependencies) built into the pages:

- **A/B sample-size planner** - drag baseline rate, MDE, alpha, power, and traffic; watch the
  required sample size and runtime update live.
- **The peeking penalty** - run hundreds of simulated A/A tests and watch checking-every-day
  inflate the false-positive rate far above the nominal 5%.

## The gap this course owns

Deep research across DeepLearning.AI, Coursera, 365 Data Science, LinkedIn Learning, and Udemy
found that no single course covers the modern online-experiment stack. Hypothesis testing,
power, and propensity scores are taught widely; **sequential testing / peeking, CUPED, and
synthetic control are a gap everywhere.** This course teaches that trio from the primary sources
(Johari et al. 2017, Deng-Xu-Kohavi 2013, Abadie 2003/2010).
